Who is responsible

Yi-Ting Yang Tang, 3D modeling & texturing artist, Madrid (Spain). Contact: lisayitingyang@gmail.com. This website is a personal portfolio: it sells nothing and has no accounts, no ads and no newsletter.

What data I receive

Only what you type in the contact form: name, email, subject and message. They are sent by a server function hosted on Vercel and delivered to my Gmail inbox (Google), and you get an automatic acknowledgement at the email you wrote. Nothing is stored in a database on this website. To limit spam, your IP address is held in memory for about one minute to count submissions, and the hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operation.

If you write to me directly by email, I receive the data your email contains.

Why and on what legal basis

To read and answer your message, which you ask for by sending it (consent and pre-contractual measures, art. 6.1.a and 6.1.b GDPR). I do not use it for anything else, I do not sell it and I do not send you anything you did not ask for.

How long

The time needed to answer and keep the conversation, and afterwards only while it is useful for the working relationship or required by law. You can ask me to delete it at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no localStorage or similar storage, and has no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. For that reason there is no cookie banner. Fonts and scripts are served from this same site, so your browser does not contact third parties just by loading it. If you click an outgoing link (ArtStation, LinkedIn), the privacy policy of that site applies.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or objection by writing to lisayitingyang@gmail.com. Send the request from the same email you used and I will answer within one month. If you think your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD, aepd.es).

This website is not aimed at children under 14 and I do not knowingly collect their data.
